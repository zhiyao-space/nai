What is NovelAI?
NovelAI (Official Website: novelai.net) is a subscription-based platform centered on AI anime image generation and interactive text creation, developed by Anlatan Inc. in the US. The platform’s biggest feature is its use of end-to-end encryption to protect user creations, offering image generation models specifically built for anime enthusiasts (NAI Diffusion v3) and AI text models that support long-form novel writing.
In March 2026, NovelAI launched its latest text model, Xialong. Based on ZhipuAI’s GLM-4.6 model and fine-tuned with NovelAI’s exclusive creative dataset, it significantly enhances creativity and consistency in storytelling. It is currently available only to Opus subscribers.
6 Core Features of NovelAI
1. AI Anime Image Generation (NAI Diffusion v3)
NovelAI’s image generation engine is the highlight of the platform. Its self-developed NAI Diffusion v3 model is optimized specifically for anime styles, capable of generating high-quality anime characters and scenes. It supports Multi-Character Prompts and provides a rich visual tag system, allowing users to precisely control details like style, pose, and clothing.
2. Vibe Transfer
Vibe Transfer is an exclusive NovelAI feature that allows users to extract the style of a favorite image and use it as a visual inspiration base for new images. This achieves style consistency easily without complex ControlNet settings, making it perfect for creators making fan art series.
3. Image Post-Processing Toolkit
NovelAI provides a complete set of post-processing tools, including: Inpaint , Image2Image, Remove BG, Line Art , Sketch, Colorize, Emotion (expression adjustment), and Declutter, completing the full workflow from generation to refinement in one place.
4. AI Interactive Fiction Engine
The text creation module offers an immersive interactive novel writing experience, supporting various AI text models (up to Xialong). Combined with the Lorebook  feature, the AI can remember complex world-building, character personalities, and plot threads. Users can freely set the narrative style, perspective, and tone of the story.
5. End-to-End Encryption for Privacy
NovelAI places a strong emphasis on privacy protection. All stories and images created by users are protected by end-to-end encryption, ensuring that even the platform itself cannot read private creations. This is rare among AI creation platforms and is highly favored by privacy-conscious creators.
6. Random Prompt Inspiration
For creators facing writer’s block, NovelAI provides a random prompt dice feature, generating brand new image prompts with one click. Combined with the real-time preview in the image generation interface, users can quickly iterate and explore different visual styles.
NovelAI Subscription Plans & Pricing
NovelAI offers three subscription tiers, plus a free trial (30 free image generations):
Tablet ($10/mo) : 1,000 Anlas per month, basic image generation, unlimited AI text generation, up to 1024×1024 resolution.
Scroll ($15/mo) : 1,000 Anlas per month + Unlimited image generation*, higher text memory limit, image generation priority queue.
Opus ($25/mo) : 10,000 Anlas per month + Unlimited image generation*, latest Xialong text model, highest resolution, maximum memory limit, priority support.
*Unlimited image generation is limited to single images under 1024×1024 resolution and within 28 steps. Generations exceeding these specs consume Anlas. On-demand Anlas purchases are also available without a subscription.
