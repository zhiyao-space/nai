NovelAI's Core Features
The platform gives you a complete toolkit for anime creation, from quick brainstorming to polished final outputs. Here's how each feature serves your creative needs.
The Creative Editor  is the heart of NovelAI—a purpose-built interface that keeps you focused on creation rather than wrestling with complicated tools. The intuitive layout means you can start generating images almost immediately, whether you're a first-time user or a seasoned digital artist. The editor works seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices, so you can create on your laptop at home or make quick adjustments on your phone.
Image Adjustment  gives you two powerful ways to refine existing images.  Image2Image  lets you upload a reference photo or sketch and transform it into anime style while preserving the original composition—perfect for converting concept art or bringing traditional sketches into the digital realm.  Enhance  adds detail and depth to existing images, upgrading low-resolution drafts into portfolio-ready pieces.
When creative block hits,  Random Prompt  is your instant inspiration boost. Just click the dice button, and the system generates a unique prompt combining different character traits, outfits, settings, and moods. Use it as a starting point, or let surprise elements spark entirely new directions.
For users who want precise control,  Advanced AI Settings  provide granular parameters:
Steps  (default 23): Controls how many refinement passes the AI performs Guidance  (default 5): Determines how closely the output follows your prompt Sampler : Choose between options like Euler Ancestral and Euler for different aesthetic outcomes Seed : Lock in specific random patterns for reproducible results
Multiple Character Prompts  let you design complex compositions where different characters have distinct appearances, outfits, and expressions. Each character gets its own prompt section, giving you precise control over individual elements within the same image.
Vibe Transfer  analyzes a reference image's aesthetic DNA—its color relationships, line quality, shading approach, and artistic mood—then applies those characteristics to your new generation. This is invaluable for artists building a consistent portfolio or creators who want their fan art to match the visual language of their favorite series.
